# GTM Launch Plan, Riverty business-unit case

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Feature | "Why we ask" on every income question (addresses research theme that consumers don't understand why information is requested) |
| Goal | Engagement |
| Launch tier | S, Minimal |

## Goal & Audience
- **Goal:** Engagement, Encourage the users to add the information we ask for
- **Target audience:** Role: The consumer Mara, a shift worker with two open claims and variable but stable income; Goal: Agree an instalment plan she can actually afford and keep, without making her situation worse; Friction: She opens the portal after a reminder and hits the income and affordability questions. She doesn't know why they're asked, what an affordable plan looks like, whether a wrong answer or a missed payment will make things worse, or whether both claims can be handled together. Unable to find the right documents or get answers, she gives up and calls an agent. This also fits your "panic" angle: the fear of consequences is the trigger.

## Launch Tier
- **S, Minimal**, We're only adjusting content in an already excisting feature

## Channels
1. **Owned: Consumer Portal**
2. **Owned: E-Mail with deeplink to the Portal**
3. **Owned: Letter**

## Enablement & Assets
Short demo of how to set up a instalment that match your income or add info in any of the exciting FAQ.

## Ownership, Budget & Timeline
- **Ownership & budget:** Plan step, sentence, and rule: the product manager of this PRD, one person.
Build in the portal: one engineering lead, one person.
One-pager for the call: one person in collections who briefs Daniel. The case study names no individuals.
No media spend. The extra cost is the briefing and the measurement of the call within 7 days and the keep rate at 60 days. Neither is visible today. Do not build: paid campaigns, influencers, a model, an image check, reminders, or a launch outside the Germany pilot.
- **Timeline:** Phase 1, beta: 50/50 in the one segment, 14 days of enrollment plus 7 days of follow-up. Do not ship on day 21.
Phase 2, launch: only once, 60 days after agreement, the keep rate in the variant is at least 81% and the call rate has fallen by at least 5 percentage points below 31%. Then in the portal only, for this segment.
Phase 3, after launch: keep reading the keep rate, with the one-pager in use. No further market while the 81% holds.

## Success Metrics
- **Metrics:** Share of starters who contact an agent within 7 days. Warning sign: the rate falls and the 60-day keep rate drops below 81%.
Share who see the comfortable instalment and the sentence before they confirm. Warning sign: confirmations rise while the sentence was not shown.
Share of new agreements kept through 60 days, floor 81%. Warning sign: the miss rate rises above 19%.
- **Bad signal to watch for:** Many people see the instalment, and the call rate stays at 31%. Or calls fall and the 60-day miss rate rises. Then she agreed without being able to keep the instalment.
- **Likely post-launch decision:** Repeat. Trigger: the call rate falls by less than 5 percentage points, or it falls and the keep rate is below 81%. Double only if both are true: at least 5 percentage points fewer calls and a keep rate of at least 81%. Lower the priority if the call rate does not fall or it rises.
